GROUP STAGE
Points for each match:                                                              
1 pt - Correctly predict the outcome of a match (outcome means which team wins/loses, or if the match is a draw).
1 pt - Correctly predict the exact score of a match.                                        
   
Points for Group order:  
4 pts - Correctly predict the 1st place team in a group.                                      
5 pts - Correctly predict the 1st place team in a group + any other rank (1st and last, 1st and 2nd or 1st and 3rd)
9 pts - Correctly predict the entire group's order (1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th place)

KNOCKOUT STAGE
Round-of-32                                                                              
3 pts -  Correctly predict the exact score of a Round-of-32 match  ("exact score" refers to the score before penalty shootout)      
                                                                                       
Round-of-16                                                                              
10 pts -  For each team correctly predicted to reach the Round-of-16.                                      
4 pts -  Correctly predict exact score of a Round-of-16 match.  (must have predicted that both teams would face each other)        
                                                                                       
Quarter-Finals                                                                              
20 pts -  For each team correctly predicted to reach the Quarterfinals.                                      
8 pts -  Correctly predict exact score of a Quarterfinals match.  (must have predicted that both teams would face each other)        
                                                                                       
Semi-Finals                                                                              
40 pts -  For each team correctly predicted to reach the Semifinals.                                        
16 pts -  Correctly predict exact score of a Semifinals match.  (must have predicted that both teams would face each other)        
                                                                                       
Consolation score predictions not in scope for this pool, however the goals and cards are still counted  for bonus questions.  
                                                                                       
Finals                                                                                  
80 pts -  For each team correctly predicted to reach the Finals.                                            
32 pts -  Correctly predict exact score of a Finals match.  (must have predicted that both teams would face each other)            
120 pts -  Correctly predict the World Cup Champion.
